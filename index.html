Chocolate Cake Recipe

Learn the easiest way to bake a chocolate cake

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups of all purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons of baking powder
  • 1.5 teaspoons of bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract (optional)
  • 2 Eggs
  • vanilla extract
  • 2 Cups of caster sugar
  • a little oil or butter, for greasing the pan
  • 1 cup of black coffee
  • 1/2 cup of cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup of vegetable oil
  • 1 cup of milk

Instructions

  1. Turn on your oven and set it to 180°C (350°F) to heat up.
  2. Grease your baking pan with a little oil or butter, then dust it lightly with flour or cocoa powder.
  3. Take a large mixing bowl.
  4. Add your 2 cups of all-purpose flour, 2 cups of caster sugar, 1/2 cup of cocoa powder, 2 tsp of baking powder, and 1.5 tsp of bicarbonate of soda.
  5. Whisk or stir them well so they combine evenly.
  6. Add 2 eggs, 1 cup of milk, 1/2 cup of vegetable oil, and vanilla extract (if using) into the dry mixture.
  7. Beat or mix well until the batter is smooth.
  8. Stir gently until the batter becomes smooth and slightly thin.
  9. Pour your batter into your prepared baking pan.
  10. Place it in the oven and bake for 35 to 40 minutes.
  11. Test if it is ready by poking a toothpick into the center; if it comes out clean, the cake is done.
